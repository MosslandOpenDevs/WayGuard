A refined mobile home screen for Safety Guardian that keeps the map dominant and makes the product feel community-powered, calm, and trustworthy.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Mobile web app
- Device: Narrow mobile canvas, iPhone-style proportions
- Theme: Light-first, calm, trustworthy, neighborhood-focused
- Background: Soft gray app canvas with bright white elevated surfaces
- Primary Accent: Safety Blue (#2764e7)
- Secondary Accent: Emerald (#10b981)
- Warning Accent: Amber (#f59e0b)
- Danger Accent: Red (#ef4444)
- Typography: Inter-like modern sans serif with bold compact headers
- Surfaces: Large radius cards, soft blur when floating, whisper-light shadows
- Interaction Style: Practical and reassuring, not flashy
- Product Framing: Community-powered safety tool, not a government dashboard
- Keep: Wide map canvas, floating summary card docked low, pill filters, clear bottom nav
- Avoid: Purple neon, enterprise admin patterns, dense tables, surveillance-heavy language, public-open-data framing

**Page Structure:**
1. **Header:** App title, avatar, and a lightweight neighborhood search field.
2. **Filter Row:** Resident-first filters for all activity, reports, community signals, safe routes, and safe hubs.
3. **Map Canvas:** A wide map that visually dominates the screen with subtle floating zoom and current-location controls.
4. **Summary Card:** A low-docked floating card titled around current-location safety confidence, not public infrastructure counts.
5. **Metrics:** Community signals, safe-route activity, and resident reports.
6. **Bottom Navigation:** Stable bottom nav with the safe-return center action clearly visible.

**Specific Goals:**
- Make the map feel wider than the current implementation.
- Lower the summary card so it feels docked just above the bottom navigation.
- Use Korean copy for all visible labels.
- Avoid CCTV, streetlight, or emergency-bell framing as the main story.
- Make the screen feel useful in one glance.
