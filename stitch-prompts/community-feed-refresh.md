A mobile community feed for Safety Guardian.

Create a local neighborhood feed where residents share useful safety signals and short updates. The screen should feel more local and civic than generic-social.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Mobile web app
- Theme: Light, social, local, practical
- Primary Accent: Safety Blue (#2764e7)
- Surface: White cards on a soft gray canvas
- Typography: Compact, easy-to-scan mobile hierarchy

**Page Structure:**
1. Sticky header with neighborhood title and filter pills.
2. Feed of resident posts with clear author, time, and location context.
3. Light reaction and reply affordances.
4. Floating write button.
5. Bottom-sheet composer for new posts.

**Specific Goals:**
- Use Korean copy for all visible labels.
- Keep posts readable and local, not influencer-like.
- Emphasize neighborhood usefulness, not vanity metrics.
- The composer should feel quick and inviting.
