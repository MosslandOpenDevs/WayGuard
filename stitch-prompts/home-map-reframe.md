A refined mobile home screen for Safety Guardian.

This is the main map screen for a Korean neighborhood safety app. The screen must feel community-powered, calm, and useful at a glance. Do not frame the product as a government dashboard or open-data portal.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Mobile web app
- Theme: Light-first, trustworthy, quiet, practical
- Background: Soft gray app canvas (#f6f6f8)
- Primary Accent: Safety Blue (#2764e7) for active states and core actions
- Secondary Accent: Emerald (#10b981) for safe or positive signals
- Warning Accent: Amber (#f59e0b) for cautionary reference data
- Danger Accent: Red (#ef4444) for urgent reports
- Typography: Inter-like compact sans serif with strong hierarchy
- Surfaces: Large rounded cards, soft blur, soft shadow, bright white surfaces

**Page Goal:**
Reframe the home experience around neighborhood participation instead of public infrastructure.

**Page Structure:**
1. Header with app title, avatar, and a lightweight search field.
2. Horizontal pill filter row for all activity, resident reports, community signals, safe routes, and safe hubs.
3. Large map canvas that dominates the screen.
4. Floating zoom controls and current-location control on the map.
5. Low-docked summary card above the bottom navigation.
6. Bottom navigation with a stronger center action for safe return.

**Visual Direction:**
- The map should feel wider and more breathable than the current version.
- The summary card should sit very low, almost docked above the bottom navigation.
- Marker styles should feel human and community-oriented.
- Use Korean copy for the visible labels.
- Remove any visual story built around CCTV counts, streetlights, or emergency bells.

**Content Tone:**
- Community-driven
- Practical
- Reassuring
- Contemporary
